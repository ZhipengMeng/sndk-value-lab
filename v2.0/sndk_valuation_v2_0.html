What the audit changed

The old $2,750 midpoint depended heavily on exceptional margins lasting. Its terminal economic operating margin was 74.50%; 58–60% of enterprise value came from the terminal calculation. Limiting that margin to 50% alone lowers the old midpoint to $2,099. The full revised central case also changes the operating path, capital budgets, timing and equity bridge; its effects are not additive to that one-change test.

Verified evidence

FY2026 adjusted FCF reconciles as $11.671B operating cash − $0.177B direct capex − $0.275B net JV funding − $2.476B NBM receipts = $8.743B.

JV fixed-cost payments and incremental investment funding remain obligations. Building prepayments receive future wafer-purchase credits. The combined $31B investment plan is not a Sandisk net-cash schedule.

Assumptions now explicit

Management’s 80% GM, 75% OM and 50% adjusted-FCF targets cover average FY2028–30 performance. The annual allocation, timing of NAND price erosion and terminal 50% margin are model choices.

Customer advances are reserved, SBC is charged, and marketable investments are valued separately. Future buybacks are not inferred from authorization.

The cash proxy is an operating cash approximation, not a complete future GAAP cash-flow reconciliation. Its unexplained residual is visible as an unallocated allowance.

Forward earnings and P/E

Q1 FY2027 guidance midpoints: $10.55B sales and $45 adjusted EPS. They are quarterly figures; four times Q1 EPS is not the annual consensus. The first modeled annual income statement is anchored to external FY2027 estimates.

Economic cash flow ($B)

Economic FCFF = after-tax operating income after SBC + own-company D&A − direct capex − net JV funding − Δ working capital − unallocated cash allowance. Customer deposits and portfolio gains are excluded.
